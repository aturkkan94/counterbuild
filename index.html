How it works

Meta builds

Champion and role pages with tier lists, core items, item order and rune pages, computed from aggregated ranked matches.

Counter picks

In champion select, suggestions against your likely lane opponent and both team compositions. Nothing is picked for you.

Explained item choices

A small window can highlight an item decision with options and the mechanical reason behind it. You decide; nothing is bought or applied automatically.

Data & privacy

  • Riot Games API: ACCOUNT-V1, SUMMONER-V4, LEAGUE-V4 and MATCH-V5 (matches and timelines) are used on our server to compute aggregate statistics (EUW, ranked solo queue). Only aggregates are published; no individual player data is displayed.
  • No player identifiers published: published datasets contain no PUUIDs or Riot IDs.
  • On your computer: the desktop app reads the local League client and the local Live Client Data API only on your machine. It does not send your game data anywhere.
  • Game integrity: no enemy cooldown or timer tracking, no hidden information, no automation of game decisions. Rune and item-set imports happen only when you click.
  • Usage analytics: off by default; optional and opt-in.
  • API key: stays on our server and is never shipped in the app.
